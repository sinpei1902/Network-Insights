Typical key performance insights (what you’ll aim to surface)
- Overall availability and SLA breaches (e.g., X% of links failed to meet 99.9% during the period).
- High-latency corridors and peak-hour degradation (e.g., p95 latency increased by Y ms during business hours).
- Persistent packet loss on specific interfaces or paths.
- Trending capacity issues: links or devices approaching sustained >70–80% utilization.
- Correlation between recent configuration/firmware changes and incident spikes.
- Geographic or application-specific impact (e.g., branch offices or VoIP traffic more affected).

Common trends and problem areas to look for
- Diurnal patterns: repeated congestion during work hours indicates capacity or QoS misconfiguration.
- Growing trend of utilization on core links — capacity planning needed in 1–3 months.
- Spikes in retransmits or errors after upgrades — configuration rollback or misapplied change.
- “Noisy” devices: repeated reboots, errors, or memory leaks causing intermittent outages.
- Single points of failure: links or devices with no redundancy causing long MTTR.
- Monitoring gaps: missing telemetry or delayed metric collection hindering rapid detection.
